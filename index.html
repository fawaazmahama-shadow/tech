Privacy notice

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Who I am

Tech Labs is run by Mahama Fawaaz Alelemato in Accra, Ghana. Contact: fawaazmahama@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, the service you are interested in, and your message.
  • Accounts: your name and email. If you sign up with a password, it is stored in scrambled (hashed) form by the sign-in provider and I cannot read it. If you use Google sign-in, I receive your Google name and email.
  • Project requests: the title, service type and budget you enter, and any messages you send in a project discussion.

Why

To reply to you, prepare quotes, deliver your project and let you follow its progress. I do not sell your data and I do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only me. Your data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those providers may process technical data such as your IP address.

How long I keep it

For as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or project and to follow up afterwards. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you by emailing me. I handle personal data with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind.

Cookies and storage

The site keeps your sign-in session and your light or dark preference in your browser. There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.
